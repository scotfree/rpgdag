# Highway Magic

The game is a crossing of mythic 1976 America on the interstate highway system. It begins at an Iggy Pop and the Stooges concert on the east coast and ends at a culminating ritual atop Mount Oumalouma on the coast of California. Players are members of a high school band trying to make it to a big performance. Locations are cities, edges are highways.

Major axis: Technology vs Magic.

Dynamic elements include a bus of hippies becoming a Death Witch Cult, a motorcycle gang, and government agents.
